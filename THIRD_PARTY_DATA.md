# Source attribution and reuse conditions

This release contains original software and selected derived numerical summaries,
not the original study archives. The complete source registry is
`paper/source_releases.yaml`; licenses in that registry are source-specific.

The principal two-study timing analysis uses:

- Lencioni et al. (2019), *Human kinematic, kinetic and EMG data during different
  walking and stair ascending and descending tasks*, Scientific Data.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0323-z.
- Gait120, *Comprehensive Human Locomotion and Electromyography Dataset*.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-05391-0;
  dataset https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.27677016.v1 (CC BY 4.0).

The stored participant profiles are computed EMG envelopes, time-normalized and
scaled within the protocol; they are not untouched raw recordings. Their source
study, participant code, processing condition and aggregation are retained. They
must not be interpreted as common calibrated amplitudes. Additional conversion
summaries refer to the releases and attributions in the source registry.

Where source material is CC BY 4.0, give the original source credit, link to
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, and identify the transformation.
Do not treat the software's Apache-2.0 license as permission to redistribute
unbundled source datasets, articles or external model files.
